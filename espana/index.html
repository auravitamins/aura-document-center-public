The seven stages

The one thing that can strand everything Start stage 1 on Monday. The Amazon EU account and KYC are free and depend on nothing, but Amazon publishes no turnaround time and US LLCs hit predictable snags. If it stalls at month four, every euro spent on the company, the labs and the labels is sitting idle waiting for it.

Why the Spanish company changes everything

Spanish and EU law tests whether a company is established in Spain, not whether a person lives there. Right now Aura Vitamins LLC is a US company, so it cannot be named on a Spanish label, cannot file the product notification, and cannot register as a food business. A Spanish S.L. does all three, plus it removes the need for a fiscal representative and keeps liability inside a company instead of on a person.

If the Spain lead holds a Spanish DNI, that DNI already works as a tax number, which skips a consular queue that otherwise runs two weeks to three months. They can be founder and administrator while living abroad.

Two rejections that catch everyone Mercury, Wise and Payoneer statements are refused. Amazon's policy excludes online-only banks outright, so use a traditional bank statement. And do not use a registered-agent address — it triggers "your business address is used by multiple companies."

Create the regional Europe account

In Seller Central go to Sell Globally → Amazon Stores Worldwide → Link Account. You are creating a separate Europe account and linking it to the US one, not adding a marketplace to your existing account. It needs its own email address.

One Europe account covers Spain, Germany, France, Italy, the Netherlands, Poland, Sweden, Belgium and Ireland.

Amazon publishes no turnaround time. Put a weekly reminder in your calendar and do not let it go quiet.

Create the company in Spain

Form a Spanish S.L. It becomes the importer, the name on the label, the party that files with AESAN, the food business registrant and the VAT payer. Every blocker in this project exists only because this company does not exist yet.

Runs at the same time as stage 1. Do not wait for Amazon.

Confirm the Spain lead holds a Spanish DNI

If so, the DNI is already the tax number and there is no NIE application. That skips the single longest queue in company formation, normally two weeks to three months from abroad. They can be founder and administrator while living outside Spain.

JimmyOne message

Agree his role in writing, before formation

Sign a commission agreement that pays nothing until first revenue. That keeps cash where you want it, but documented. The Spain lead is about to carry a real legal role for four to six months with no money coming in, and an unpaid arrangement will not survive that.

Keep equity with Aura Vitamins LLC at 100% or 90/10, and keep any stake the Spain lead holds under one third. Put exit terms in the same page.

What administrador actually means It is a legal role, not a title. A Spanish administrador becomes personally liable for company debts that arise after a dissolution cause appears if they fail to act within two months, and courts treat failure to file annual accounts as evidence against them. Manageable, but whoever takes the role should know before they accept.

Engage one Spanish gestoría for the whole job

Ask for a fixed quote covering formation plus everything in stage 3. One relationship is far cheaper and easier than five.

ES lead1 week to choose

Reserve the name and sign the deed

Reserve the company name at the Registro Mercantil Central — submit three or four options, first choices are often taken. Then either CIRCE express, which constitutes the company in 24 to 48 hours on standard statutes, or a conventional notarial deed, 5 to 15 business days from signature to registry.

If the Spain lead signs remotely, use an apostilled power of attorney to the gestoría. Videoconference incorporation is also permitted, but the POA is the dependable route.

gestoría1–3 weeks

Capitalise at €3,000, not €1

The legal minimum is €1, but below €3,000 you must put 20% of profits into a legal reserve and the partners are jointly liable for the shortfall if the company is wound up. The €3,000 is working capital you still own, not a fee.

Stage 3•Weeks 3–8•Owner: gestoría

Register the company for everything

Five registrations. Hand them to the gestoría as one brief and they mostly run in parallel.

Needs stage 2 finished. Everything here requires the CIF.

RGSEAA — the food business register, clave 26

The sanitary register for food companies. Register for the activities you will actually do: importing, manufacturing, packaging, storing or distributing supplements.

This is on the critical path. The AESAN product notification in stage 6 will not be accepted without it.

gestoríaCritical path

VAT registration, then the ROI step

File Modelo 036 to register for VAT. Then file a separate ROI application so the number becomes valid on VIES. Amazon validates against VIES, so the plain domestic number is not enough.

The ROI step is routinely forgotten and it is a separate application. Ask the gestoría to confirm it is done.

gestoría2 steps, not 1
Good news on the rate Supplements qualifying as complementos alimenticios are taxed at 10% VAT, not 21%. Worth eleven points of gross on every unit. Ask the gestoría to confirm it for your specific products.

Packaging: join a SCRAP first, then get the ENV number

Join Ecoembes or an equivalent scheme first, because the membership certificate is required at the moment you register. Then obtain the ENV number from MITECO.

There is no small-producer exemption. The obligation starts at the first unit sold, and Amazon suspends listings without the number.

gestoría~€300–475/yr

File the EUIPO trademark

Your US trademark does not work on Amazon.es. You need a European mark from EUIPO or the Spanish office. Without it you lose A+ content, Sponsored Brands, Vine reviews and Brand Analytics.

Amazon's IP Accelerator grants Brand Registry on a pending application if you need it sooner. Note this may be a new name — see stage 4.

JimmyFile early

EORI number — only if you import

Needed only if you go the import route in stage 5. Skip it if you manufacture in Spain.

Stage 4•Week 1, free•Owner: Jimmy

Choose the right products

Pick two or three products to launch. This costs nothing, takes an afternoon, and it is the cheapest place to kill a bad idea. Do it in week one.

Runs in parallel with stages 1 and 2. No company needed to make this decision.
Read this first In the EU the brand name is itself a claim. A product name that suggests a health benefit can only be used if the label also carries an officially authorised claim. The old grandfathering for existing brands expired in January 2022, so there is no way around it. This is why your top three US sellers are the wrong three to take.

Your top 3, assessed

ProductVerdictWhy
Vitiligo Purple CreamLeave out of SpainA cream is a cosmetic, a completely different regime from supplements — different notification, different consultant, different factories. And vitiligo is a disease, so claiming repigmentation makes it a medicine needing an authorisation you will not get.
Audio CalmRename itThere is no authorised EU claim for hearing or tinnitus, so there is nothing to pair the name with. The formula may be fine. You already have a second label for Ecuador, so new artwork is not a big deal.
BactirureCheck the nameSpain does allow the word probiótico on labels, which is more permissive than most of Europe. But no authorised claim links probiotics to any benefit, and if the name reads as bacteria plus cure that is a medical claim.

Start from the EU authorised claims list, not your sales rank

Open the EU Register of authorised health claims and find which of your existing formulas map onto a claim already on it. Roughly 222 claims exist, mostly for vitamins, minerals and fatty acids.

A Cal-Mag Zinc type formula is a far better first Spanish product than any of your top three, because calcium, magnesium and zinc all carry clean authorised claims you can print without an argument.

Run the auto-kill checklist

Anything on this list is out before you look further: melatonin at 2 mg or more is a medicine in Spain; ephedra and yohimbe are banned; red yeast rice must stay under 3 mg monacolins; green tea under 800 mg EGCG; NMN, CBD and ashwagandha leaf are unauthorised novel foods.

Also check anything animal derived — fish oil, collagen, gelatin capsules, whey, glucosamine. These need veterinary border clearance if imported, which is a good reason to make them in Spain instead.

JimmyFree public lists

Pick new EU names for what survives

Names that describe the product without promising an outcome. Do this before stage 3's trademark filing so you register the right name once.

Why the Spanish shelf looks so boring 67% of the Amazon.es bestseller top thirty is magnesium or creatine, and the median price is about €19. That is not a coincidence. Commodity vitamins and minerals dominate because they are the only products whose benefits map to an authorised claim, so they are the only ones anyone can legally advertise. Your US products sell on claims that are illegal there.

Send the quote request to 6–8 labs

Expect three or four useful replies. Spanish labs are generally more flexible on minimums for new brands than Asian ones, and some run very small first batches. Start with Europages filtered to Spain, plus Formulaceutics, Fabricantes de Suplementos, Happy Nutri Lab, Barosa Labs and Avanceutics in Seville.

Ask all of them the same questions so the quotes are comparable.

Insist on the ISO 17025 certificate in the quote

Amazon gates the supplements category and asks for a lab certificate of analysis from an accredited lab. If the manufacturer includes it you avoid a separate test at roughly $250 per product. Most will say yes if you ask up front, and it is awkward to add later.

Stage 6•Weeks 10–16•Owner: lab + ES lead

Label and notify

Get a legal Spanish label onto the product and tell the authorities it exists. Strictly sequential — you cannot print a label until the company exists to be named on it.

Needs stage 2 and stage 5. The company's name and address are a mandatory part of the label.

Build the Spanish label

It must carry: the words "Complemento Alimenticio", your S.L.'s name and address, nutrients per recommended daily dose with %NRV, the four mandatory warnings, allergens, lot number and date.

Castilian Spanish is the legal minimum. Only claims from the authorised EU list may appear.

File the AESAN notification

Submit with a copy of the label, on or before the first sale. Made in Spain, this goes to your autonomous community. Costs roughly €60 to €126 per product.

There is nothing to wait for. You may sell from the moment you file — there is no approval step. But skipping it is a serious offence, so file it.

Write the listing natively in Spanish

Written for Spanish search, not translated from English. Current limits are much tighter than they used to be: title 75 characters, bullets 10 to 255 characters each, backend keywords under 250 bytes — accented characters like ñ and á cost two bytes each. There is also a new 125-character Item Highlights field that is indexed for search.

Also affects your US listings The 75-character title cap applies globally, not just in Europe. Your existing SEO standard of near-max titles and 350–480 character bullets is now over the limit everywhere. Worth a separate pass across the whole catalog.

Put the label information on the detail page

In the EU the product page is legally part of the label. Ingredients, allergens, warnings and the company's address must all appear before checkout, not just on the bottle.
