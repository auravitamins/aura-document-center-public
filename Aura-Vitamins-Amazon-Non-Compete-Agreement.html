NON-COMPETE, NON-CIRCUMVENTION AND BRAND PROTECTION AGREEMENT

Amazon channel · Ancillary to the Amazon Distribution Agreement between the Parties

This Non-Compete, Non-Circumvention and Brand Protection Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of , 20 (the "Effective Date"), by and between Aura Vitamins LLC, a Florida limited liability company ("Supplier"), and , a company ("Distributor").

WHEREAS the Parties entered into an Amazon Distribution Agreement dated (the "Distribution Agreement") for (the "Authorized Product"); WHEREAS Distributor may receive access to Supplier's brands, catalog, pricing, supplier identities, and commercial information, including through a Brand Registry selling role; and WHEREAS such access is granted only in reliance on the covenants in this Agreement; NOW THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises herein, the authorization granted under the Distribution Agreement, and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree:

1. Definitions

Brands: Supplier's brand names, trade names, logos, and trade dress, including "Aura Vitamins," "Celulas Madre," and "Aura Dermacare," whether or not registered, and any application or registration relating to them. Competing Product: any dietary supplement or topical product that is substantially similar in formulation, function, or intended use to any Supplier product that Distributor sold, listed, or gained non-public knowledge of through the Parties' relationship. Confidential Information: has the meaning given in the Distribution Agreement and includes Supplier's sales data, pricing, costs, margins, advertising and keyword data, catalog structure, formulations, and the identities of Supplier's manufacturers, formulators, laboratories, and suppliers (the "Restricted Suppliers"). Selling Role: any Amazon Brand Registry selling role (including the Reseller role) assigned to Distributor for any of the Brands. Restricted Period: the term of the Distribution Agreement plus twelve (12) months after its expiration or termination for any reason.

2. Acknowledgments

Distributor acknowledges that: (a) Supplier has legitimate business interests under Section 542.335, Florida Statutes, including trade secrets, valuable confidential business information, substantial relationships with the Restricted Suppliers and with customers, and goodwill associated with the Brands and with Supplier's Amazon listings; (b) the access and authorization Distributor receives would cause Supplier irreparable competitive harm if used outside this Agreement; (c) Supplier owns all rights in the Brands regardless of the registration status of any trademark application, and Distributor will not challenge, oppose, or assist any challenge to Supplier's ownership, use, or registration of the Brands anywhere; and (d) the restrictions in this Agreement do not prevent Distributor from earning a living in its general line of business.

3. Non-Compete

During the Restricted Period, Distributor shall not, directly or indirectly, develop, manufacture, private-label, import, list, market, or sell on the Amazon.com marketplace any Competing Product, and shall not at any time use any Confidential Information to develop, source, price, or market any product that competes with any Supplier product in any channel.

4. Non-Circumvention

During the Restricted Period, Distributor shall not, directly or indirectly, contact, engage, or transact with any Restricted Supplier for the purpose of sourcing, producing, or obtaining any Competing Product or any product substantially similar to a Supplier product, and shall not induce or attempt to induce any Restricted Supplier, customer, or business relation of Supplier to end, reduce, or alter its relationship with Supplier.

5. Scope of Amazon Authorization

Distributor's authorization is limited to the Authorized Product. Whether or not any Selling Role technically permits broader access, Distributor shall not: (a) create, list, or offer any other ASIN of Supplier without Supplier's prior written approval for each ASIN; (b) create any new ASIN, variation, bundle, or multipack under any of the Brands; (c) use A+ Content, Brand Store, video, or advertising tools associated with the Brands, including Sponsored Brands, except as Supplier directs in writing; or (d) submit any edit or contribution to any Supplier listing. Distributor acknowledges that any Selling Role is granted and revocable at Supplier's sole discretion, without notice or cure period, and that revocation is not a breach of this Agreement or of the Distribution Agreement.

6. Brand Protection and No-Challenge

Distributor shall not register, attempt to register, or use any trademark, brand name, logo, domain name, storefront name, or product name identical or confusingly similar to any of the Brands; shall not create or contribute to any listing that imitates Supplier's trade dress; and shall not apply for, oppose, or interfere with any trademark application of Supplier. Any ASIN, listing content, registration, or domain created or obtained by Distributor in violation of this Section is held in trust for Supplier, and Distributor shall assign and transfer it to Supplier on demand at Distributor's cost and shall execute all documents needed to do so. Distributor shall cooperate with Supplier's enforcement efforts, including providing declarations and records.

7. No Use of Commercial Information

All sales velocity, pricing, advertising, keyword, catalog, and supplier information Distributor obtains through the relationship, including anything visible through a Selling Role, is Confidential Information. Distributor shall use it solely to perform the Distribution Agreement, shall limit access to personnel who need it, and shall not use it to compete with Supplier or share it with any third party.

8. Non-Solicitation

During the Restricted Period, Distributor shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit for employment or engagement any employee or contractor of Supplier, and shall not solicit any Restricted Supplier to produce goods competing with Supplier's products.

9. Post-Termination Obligations

Upon expiration or termination of the Distribution Agreement, or upon revocation of any Selling Role, Distributor shall immediately: (a) stop creating new offers on any Supplier ASIN; (b) send no further inventory of any Supplier product to Amazon fulfillment centers; (c) on Supplier's written request, create removal or disposal orders for Distributor's remaining FBA inventory of Supplier products within ten (10) days, except inventory Supplier elects to repurchase under the Distribution Agreement; (d) cease all use of the Brands and of any Selling Role benefit; and (e) return or destroy all Confidential Information and certify destruction in writing on request.

10. Exceptions

Nothing in this Agreement restricts: (a) products Distributor already sold before the Effective Date and listed in Exhibit A; (b) information that is or becomes public through no fault of Distributor; or (c) products developed entirely without use of Supplier's Confidential Information, as demonstrated by Distributor's written records.

11. Remedies and Liquidated Damages

A breach of this Agreement would cause Supplier irreparable harm for which money damages are inadequate, and Supplier is entitled to immediate injunctive relief without bond, in addition to all other remedies. Because the harm from unauthorized use of the Brands is real but difficult to measure, the Parties agree that for each ASIN, listing, or registration created or used in violation of Sections 5 or 6, Distributor shall pay Supplier $5,000 per violation, plus $250 per day the violation continues after written notice. The Parties agree these amounts are a reasonable pre-estimate of loss and not a penalty, and they are in addition to, not in place of, injunctive relief. The prevailing Party in any action to enforce this Agreement recovers its reasonable attorneys' fees and costs.

12. Tolling

If Distributor breaches any covenant in this Agreement, the Restricted Period is extended by the duration of the breach, up to a maximum extension of twelve (12) months.

13. Reasonableness and Judicial Modification

The Parties agree the restrictions in this Agreement are reasonable in scope, duration, and subject matter and are necessary to protect the legitimate business interests identified in Section 2. If a court finds any restriction overbroad, the court shall modify it to the maximum extent enforceable rather than void it, as provided by Section 542.335, Florida Statutes.

14. General

This Agreement is governed by Florida law, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles; exclusive venue is Broward County, Florida. It supplements, and does not replace, the Distribution Agreement and the Parties' NDA; if they conflict, the provision most protective of Supplier's Brands and Confidential Information controls. Distributor may not assign this Agreement; it binds Distributor's affiliates, successors, and any Amazon selling account Distributor owns or controls. Notices follow the Distribution Agreement. No waiver is effective unless written and signed. If any provision is unenforceable, the remainder stays in effect. This Agreement and its Exhibit are the entire agreement on this subject and may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Sections 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, 9, 11, 12, and 13 survive termination.
